# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-03

First public release.

- Reads source channels with your own Telegram account (Telethon) and publishes to your channel with a Kurigram bot or with your account.
- Translation to Arabic through a chain of AI providers with automatic failover, ending with Google Translate.
- Five layers of duplicate protection: same file, similar image or video, same text, same story in another wording or language, and an AI verdict for gray cases.
- Breaking-news priority, "unconfirmed" label, topic hashtags, optional source line, and word filters (block list and allow list).
- Source deletions and edits are followed while a post is still in the queue.
- Optional video speech transcription (ffmpeg and Groq Whisper) and text reading inside images (Gemini Vision).
- Daily report at 10 PM Iraq time.
- Owner-only control panel: status, full check, sources, settings, session extraction by phone or QR, AI providers, smart features and filters.
